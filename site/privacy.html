Privacy.

A short, plain-English note on what the contact form on this website collects and what happens to it.

What the contact form collects

The form on the contact page asks for your name, company, email address, phone number, and notes about your project. Some other fields (such as project location and service needed) help describe the request.

How the details are used

Hybridata Solutions uses the details you enter only to reply to your enquiry.

Where the details go

The form does not send anything to a server. When you submit it, it opens a pre-addressed draft in your own email app. You review the message and choose whether to send it. This website does not store what you type.

Third-party scripts and fonts

This website loads no third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from the site itself.

Asking for removal

If you have sent us details and want them removed, email mh@hybridatasolutions.com and ask.
