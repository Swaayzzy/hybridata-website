Wireless

Wi-Fi problems are usually design problems.

We use Ekahau, the industry standard for wireless engineering, to survey your space, design your network, and install every access point to spec.

  1. ModelYour facility in Ekahau Pro, with wall attenuation, ceiling height and client density, so the access point layout meets coverage and capacity targets before anything is installed.
  2. ValidateOn site with passive and active surveys: signal strength, SNR and roaming measured against the design, and any gap corrected before handoff.
  3. Hand offFull coverage, reliable roaming, and a network that holds up as you add devices. One team, from blueprint to broadcast.
